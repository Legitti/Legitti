About me



Legitti

Hey there! I go by the name Legitti, a product of the rad 90s and currently chilling somewhere in the vastness of Europe. So stoked you stumbled upon my little corner of the web, welcome aboard!

You might have come across my digital footprint in various nooks and crannies of the internet. Yep, that's me, basking in the glow of a pretty sweet online vibe. Life's a wild ride, and I'm all about embracing the things I love, constantly picking up new tricks along the way.

Let's dive into the things that make my heart sing, starting with music. I'm like a sonic wizard, conjuring vibes in genres like dubstep, edm, classical, hip-hop, soundtracks and more. Always on the lookout for fresh creative avenues to level up my skills. I keep my productions sleek, minimal, and drop-dead gorgeous. And oh, did I mention my love affair with the piano?

But wait, there's more! I'm a hardcore gamer, battling it out in Age of Empires, defusing bombs in CS2 and sharing legendary jokes with grunts in the wolrd of Halo. Steam is my virtual playground, where I collect games, items, and badges like a digital hoarder. Winning is awesome, losing is part of the game, but playing is the ultimate joy. Gamer for life!

Got burning questions or just want to shoot the breeze? Hit me up, and let's have a virtual pow-wow. Oh, and did I mention I've got a blog for the freshest updates!

Big thanks for hanging out here and remember, sharing is caring!
